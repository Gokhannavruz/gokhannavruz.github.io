Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my WikiTok Pro purchase?

Open the Pro screen (from Settings) and tap Restore in the top corner. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → WikiTok, then tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the current period. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change the language?

Tap the language button at the top of the feed or go to Settings → Language. WikiTok uses your device language by default and supports 12 languages.

How do I change my interests?

Go to Settings and tap Edit next to your interests. Your feed also learns from what you read, like and share — all on your device.

How do I open the full article?

Tap the title or summary, or swipe left on any card to open the reader.

How does offline reading work?

With WikiTok Pro, every article you save is stored on your device so you can read it without an internet connection.

Where does the content come from?

All articles and images come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons under their free licenses. WikiTok is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Wikimedia Foundation. Tap the Wikipedia button on any card to see the original page.

I found an error in an article.

Article text comes straight from Wikipedia, so corrections can be made there — open the article on Wikipedia and edit it. For problems with the app itself, email us.

What data does WikiTok collect?

No account is needed and your reading data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
