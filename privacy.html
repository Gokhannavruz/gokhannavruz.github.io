Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short: WikiTok has no accounts and we never collect your name, email or contacts. Your interests, saved articles and reading history stay on your device. To work, the app talks to Wikipedia (for articles), our own server (for anonymous community recommendations and read-aloud audio), RevenueCat and Apple (for WikiTok Pro purchases) and, in the free version, Google AdMob (for ads). Those services process some data as described below.

Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your selected language and interests
  • Articles you save, and their text if you use offline reading (WikiTok Pro)
  • Your reading history, used for the daily quiz and to personalise your feed
  • App settings such as tips you have already seen

Deleting the app removes all of this data. The only exception is the anonymous like, share and open signals described under “Community recommendations” below.

Third-party services

Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation)

Articles and images are loaded directly from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. As with any website, Wikimedia's servers receive standard request information such as your IP address. See the Wikimedia Privacy Policy. WikiTok is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Wikimedia Foundation.

Community recommendations and read-aloud (WikiTok servers)

To show articles that other readers liked, the app sends anonymous signals to our server (hosted on Cloudflare) when you like, share or open an article: the article title, its language and category, and a random identifier created by the app. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier, and it is used only to count each like once. We use these signals only in aggregate to rank popular articles. When you use read-aloud, the app requests audio for the article from the same server, which creates it with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech using only the article's public Wikipedia text.

Google AdMob (free version only)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier. In the EEA, the UK and other regions where it is required, we ask for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown. WikiTok Pro subscribers do not see ads and no ads are loaded for them. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat and Apple (WikiTok Pro)

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase information (such as product, price and dates). We never receive your name, email or payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone data
  • We do not sell your data

Your choices

  • You can change ad consent from the consent prompt in the app or your device settings.
  • On iOS you can limit ad tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can cancel WikiTok Pro at any time in your App Store account settings.
  • You can delete all locally stored data by deleting the app.

Children

WikiTok is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
